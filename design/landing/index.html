§ 00 Manifesto

We keep a drawer of things we would build if we had the hours. This is the drawer, opened.

(R)Evolution is where Mansus publishes ideas before they exist: products we sketched and shelved, system designs worked out to the last queue, positions we hold about how technology should treat people, and essays that needed more room than a post. None of it is finished. All of it is free to take.

We write each piece as a handover. If one of them keeps you up at night, it is yours now.

    A tool library for a whole street

  2. № 013 Architecture 26 min Specified

    A local-first archive that survives its company

    Every note lives on your device as plain files. Sync is a convenience, not a dependency. We walk through the CRDT choice, the storage layout, the export path, and what happens on the day the servers switch off.

    → Needs: two engineers, six months

  3. № 031 Essay 18 min Argued

    Against the roadmap

    A roadmap is a promise made to people who are not in the room. We think ideas should be released early, like seeds, and owned by whoever plants them. This is why we publish what we will never build.

    → Needs: a reader who disagrees
